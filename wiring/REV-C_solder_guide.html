LEAK_DAQ_ESP32 · DAQ REV-C · 3 × MPX5010DP + 4 × BMP280

Leak DAQ Solder Guide

One 70 × 90 mm main board with the ESP32 and ADS1115, three small MPX boards and two small BMP280 pair boards (room and box). The pins match the REV-C firmware (src/main.cpp): bus 0 on D21/D22, bus 1 on D33/D32, MPX outputs into A0–A2 through 1 kΩ, and the 5 V supply into A3 through a 10k/10k divider.

3.3 V on the ADS1115 and every BMP280. Only the MPX sensors get 5 V. The 1 kΩ in each MPX line limits current into the ADS clamp diodes if a sensor ever goes over about 3.6 V (above roughly 7.5 kPa).
Tubing decides the sign. P1, the port on the logo side, is always the higher pressure. If a channel reads negative under flow, swap that sensor's two tubes.
Firmware REV-C. Channel A reads pitot − box, B box − room, C pitot − room. The two pitot sensors (A, C) are on ADS A0 and A1 and the box sensor (B) on A2, set by ADS_IN. Runs save to logs/REV-C/. Leaving MPX C off? Set HAVE_MPX[2] to false in src/main.cpp.

Small boards

Put each MPX on its own small board so its caps sit right at its pins and its tubing stays short. Each BMP280 pair shares one 4-wire lead. Wire the address pins (SDO) on every sensor, because a floating SDO can land on either address.

Build and test order

  1. Solder the female headers, the J1–J5 headers and the bare rails. Leave the modules out.
  2. Fit R1–R3, R7, R8, C1–C6, then the insulated jumpers.
  3. Check continuity: 5V, 3V3 and GND must not touch each other. Every A-node must not touch GND.
  4. Fit the ESP32 only and plug in USB. Check about 5 V on the 5V pins of J1–J3, about 3.3 V on the 3V3 rail, and about 2.5 V at the A3 node (R7/R8).
  5. Fit the ADS1115, then plug in the MPX and sensor boards. Reset with the Serial Monitor open: the startup lines should say OK for the ADS and all four BMP280s.
  6. With every port open to the room, the ESP32 zeros itself at power-up. Then run cal A, cal B and cal C against the manometer.
